## F5 Surge forecast

- **Data:** D1 = Jan 2022-Feb 2024, Dec 2022 empty (verified in S2: 4,398 rows). Lag-12 seasonal naive is scorable on 13 months per section (Dec 2023 needs Dec 2022).
- **Conformal:** split-conformal needs n ≥ 9 residuals for 90% and n ≥ 19 for 95% ([Angelopoulos & Bates, arXiv 2107.07511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511) [R abstract]). **Change:** pool residuals across the 201 sections after scaling by each section's naive MAE; report 80/90%; measure coverage. EnbPI ([Xu & Xie, ICML 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09107) [R]) needs bootstrap ensembles and ACI ([Gibbs & Candès 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170) [R]) needs a long online sequence; both are too much for 13 points, so neither is used now.
- **Reconciliation:** [Nixtla hierarchicalforecast](https://github.com/Nixtla/hierarchicalforecast) [R] (Apache-2.0, Python): BottomUp, MinTrace, conformal. Use it rather than our own code.
- **GBM:** per-section has ~13 rows → **one global model** (~2,600 rows).
- **Chronos-2: backtest-only candidate, owner decision pending.** Allowed only if it clearly beats seasonal naive, and only if the owner confirms it is consistent with the no-LLM rule. Facts: [model card](https://huggingface.co/amazon/chronos-2) [R], Apache-2.0, 120M parameters, CPU inference, known-future covariates, arXiv 2510.15821.

## F6 Shortage warning

- **Groundwater:** CGWB wells measured 4×/year ([CGWB profile](https://cgwb.gov.in/old_website/State-Profiles/Telangana%20state.pdf) [S]); telemetric + manual series via [India-WRIS](https://indiawris.gov.in/wris/#/timeseriesdata) (JS app, not script-readable) and a CSV mirror 1987-2025 ([India Data Portal](https://ckandev.indiadataportal.com/dataset/groundwater-level/resource/f78f7b15-f325-4c39-a291-f44ad4e95334) [S], 403 to our fetch). [CGWB bulletin](https://gwdata.cgwb.gov.in/download/Bulletin/Telangana_WL%20Bulletin%20August%202025.pdf) in maintenance on 2026-10-01. D1-fetch (P1) must confirm GHMC station coverage + licence.
- **Reservoirs:** CWC weekly PDFs ([CWC](https://cwc.gov.in/hi/reservoir-level-storage-bulletin) [S]); [AIKosh daily CWC](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/datasets/details/daily_data_of_reservoir_level_of_central_water_commission_cwc.html) [R] names no reservoirs and shows a one-day range. **Build without** (Q2).

## F12 Delivery proof

- Bengaluru: "water will be supplied only after the recipient shares a unique OTP", GPS per tanker ([Logical Indian, 2025-03-20](https://thelogicalindian.com/bengaluru-launches-online-booking-for-gps-tracked-cauvery-water-tankers-ensuring-fair-prices-and-quality/) [R]).
- Mumbai: OTP "verified at the designated filling station before a tanker is dispatched" ([Goodreturns, 2026-06-09](https://www.goodreturns.in/amphtml/news/bmc-to-introduce-online-otp-based-verification-for-water-tanker-bookings-in-mumbai-after-bengaluru-1514203.html) [R]); this proves dispatch, not delivery.
- Delhi: 1,111 GPS tankers monitored centrally ([The Week, 2025-04-20](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/04/20/des43-dl-gupta-ld-tankers.html) [S]).
- No published dispute process found in any of these. **Keep** our resident cross-check; it is labelled untested.

## F13 Duplicates

- [LaBSE](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/LaBSE) [R] (Apache-2.0): **Telugu and Urdu are in the language list** ([arXiv 2007.01852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01852), PDF read). [IndicSBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11434) [S] beats LaBSE on Indic STS but has **no Urdu**. [multilingual-e5-base](https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-base) [R] (MIT, 100 languages, does not name te/ur, needs "query: " prefix).
- Romanised code-mixed text stays loosely aligned to both languages in multilingual encoders ([arXiv 2603.19771](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19771) [S]). Many SMS will be romanised Telugu/Hindi, so text stays a secondary signal; location/time/household decides.

## F14 Intake and voice

- [IndicConformer-600M-multilingual](https://huggingface.co/ai4bharat/indic-conformer-600m-multilingual) [R]: MIT, **22 languages incl. Only Hindi WER is published (13.2, Vaani); **no Telugu/Urdu WER and no CPU speed** on the card or [repo](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformerASR) [R]. **Change:** add Urdu; drop NeMo; measure WER on a small labelled set; fallback (store audio for a human) stays.

## F16 Explanations and contest

- Ranking is a transparent formula, so the counterfactual (Wachter et al. 2017) is computed exactly by re-running the order; no optimiser is needed. **Add** [Alfrink et al. 2022, Minds and Machines](https://pure.tudelft.nl/ws/files/128932646/s11023_022_09611_z.pdf) [S]: contestability throughout the lifecycle.

## F18 Ledger

- [Crosby & Wallach 2009](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity09/technical-sessions/presentation/efficient-data-structures-tamper-evident) [S]: tamper evidence needs an auditor holding a commitment. [Schneier & Kelsey 1999](https://www.schneier.com/paper-auditlogs.html) [S]: hash chain + evolving keys.
- **Add:** a daily published head hash; `key_id` per entry for **key rotation**; sequence numbers so **gaps** are detected. Wording stays "tamper-evident, not blockchain".
