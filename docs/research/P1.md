# R-P1 research decision: evidence layer F1-F4

**Checked:** 2026-10-01 · **Owner:** P1 · **Scope:** public or immediately obtainable data only

## Decision summary

| Feature | Decision | Evidence and implementation consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 Water Access Atlas | **CHANGE** | **FACT:** Census 2011 remains the only verified official ward-level source found for household water access and phone access. GHMC announced 300 wards in December 2025, and Telangana subsequently reorganized the Core Urban Region into three corporations in February 2026. **INFERENCE:** the available 145-polygon DataMeet/OSM layer is a partial historical 2016 geography, not a current ward map. **PROPOSED:** freeze the demo to `GHMC_2016_150`, version all geographies, and block a “current ward” view until official machine-readable boundaries and a crosswalk are obtained. Keep exact mandal aggregates as fallback. |
| F2 Growth adjustment | **CHANGE** | **FACT:** Open Buildings Temporal supplies annual 2016-2023 building presence/count/height, but warns about jitter, temporal instability, uncalibrated confidence and uneven height validation. WorldPop and GHS-POP provide independent gridded population estimates. **PROPOSED:** treat building change as a growth covariate, not `current households = Census households × building ratio`; compare Open Buildings, WorldPop and GHS-POP and publish the range/disagreement. |
| F3 Booking equity/map | **KEEP WITH LIMITS** | **FACT:** HMWSSB data is exact at section/month level; no public authoritative section polygons or section-to-ward crosswalk were verified. **PROPOSED:** publish section statistics as exact. Ward estimates remain approximate and must expose mapping method, distance/quality and unmatched sections. Do not use generated Voronoi cells as service boundaries. |
| F4 Under-served detector | **CHANGE** | **FACT:** under-reporting is a positive-unlabelled problem: low reports can mean low need or missing reports. Agostini et al. model spatial under-reporting, but BYM2 is a separate areal prior introduced by Riebler et al. **PROPOSED:** ship a transparent negative-binomial baseline and peer-median sensitivity first. Add a spatial latent/BYM2 variant only with a valid adjacency graph, documented priors, calibration and stability tests. Output “field check required,” never “proven neglect.” |

## Required data contract and tests

- Add `geography_version`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `source_url`, `authority`, `quality`, and `parent_unit_id`. All outputs must identify `GHMC_2016_150` as historical.
- Preserve the source geography before crosswalking. Crosswalk rows carry method (`id`, `name`, `spatial`, `manual`), score, reviewer, and one-to-many weights that sum to one. Unmatched areas remain null.
- Growth outputs contain Open Buildings, WorldPop and GHS-POP estimates separately plus an agreement/range field; no single layer becomes ground truth.
- Section-to-ward estimates contain coordinates, geocoder/source, match distance and confidence. Exact section metrics and approximate ward metrics must never share the same evidence badge.
- Evaluate F4 on semi-synthetic suppression scenarios and leave-one-area-out checks. Report precision/recall only where hidden truth is created by the test; report field-check yield after deployment. A flag must not change entitlement automatically.

## Build order changes

1. **D1:** fetch historical 2016 polygons plus official current notices; add WorldPop and GHS-POP extracts. Record retrieval date, licence, checksum and geography version.
2. **E1:** implement a versioned Census-2011-to-2016 historical crosswalk. Treat a current 300-ward/three-corporation crosswalk as blocked until official boundaries exist.
3. **E3:** replace the exact household multiplier with a multi-source growth sensitivity table.
4. **E5:** implement negative-binomial and peer-median baselines first; make spatial/BYM2 an evaluated optional model, not a claim derived from Agostini.

**R-P1 conclusion:** proceed with F1-F4 after these changes. The evidence layer is feasible for a historically anchored demo, but it cannot claim current ward-level water need or prove under-reporting from booking data alone.
