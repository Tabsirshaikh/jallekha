### Subject: Application under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 seeking information regarding Section-wise Scheduled Supply Timings, SCADA Supply Hours & Flow (2024–2026), and O&M Section GIS Boundaries.

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am a citizen of India and I hereby request information under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005. The specific details required are set forth below:

#### 1. Background Context
The HMWSSB maintains an official web portal for "Water Supply Timings" located at `https://bms.hyderabadwater.gov.in/wstl/managerupload/DivisionMap.aspx`. However, an audit conducted on 2026-10-06 indicates that every section link on this portal currently returns `alert('No Data Found')`, meaning the live public database is non-operational or empty. Furthermore, links on the official board website (`https://www.hyderabadwater.gov.in/en/`) to the "Water Supply Outage Dashboard" (`https://erp.hyderabadwater.gov.in/HmwssbOnlineNew/#/OutageDashboard`) require internal ERP credentials and are not accessible to citizens, while the "Water Pressure Dashboard" (`https://mdm.hyderabadwater.gov.in/real-time-water-pressure`) is currently unreachable.

#### 2. Specific Information Requested

1. **Current Scheduled Supply Timings (Section & Locality Wise):**
   - Certified copy (in digital/spreadsheet format: CSV/Excel) of the current scheduled water supply timetable for all Operation & Maintenance (O&M) sections across all divisions in HMWSSB jurisdiction.
   - For each section and locality, please provide:
     a) Division Name and Division Code  
     b) Section Name and Section Code  
     c) Locality / Colony Name and Record ID  
     d) Slum classification (Slum / Non-Slum / Both)  
     e) Scheduled From Time and To Time (with AM/PM or 24-hour notation)  
     f) Scheduled duration in hours  
     g) Supply pattern / frequency (e.g., Daily, Alternate Day, Once in 3 Days)  
     h) Water supply source (e.g., Singoor, Manjeera, Krishna Phase I/II/III, Godavari).  

2. **Historical SCADA Operational Supply Hours and Flow Volume (2024 – 2026):**
   - Certified electronic data (CSV/Excel) of actual piped water supply delivered per O&M section, as recorded by HMWSSB's SCADA, bulk flow meters, and telemetry systems for the period from **1st January 2024 to 30th September 2026**.
   - For each section by date (or month):
     a) Section Name and Division  
     b) Actual supply hours delivered  
     c) Measured water flow / volume supplied (in Million Litres per Day, MLD, or Kilolitres)  
     d) Days of scheduled supply missed or disrupted.  

3. **Digital GIS Boundary Maps (O&M Sections and Divisions):**
   - Certified digital GIS vector boundary files (Shapefile `.shp`, GeoJSON `.geojson`, or ESRI File Geodatabase) representing the administrative boundaries of all HMWSSB Operation & Maintenance (O&M) sections, subdivisions, and divisions as cut/re-cut in 2024–2026.
   - Associated attribute table containing Section Code, Section Name, Division Number, and Sub-division Name.

4. **Public Dashboard Accessibility Status:**
   - The official URL, API specification, or public access protocol through which citizens may view real-time data from:
     a) The **Water Supply Outage Dashboard**  
     b) The **Water Pressure Dashboard** (Real-Time Water Pressure Monitoring System)  
   - If these dashboards are currently restricted to authorized personnel, please state the specific grounds, executive orders, or file references under which public access has been restricted.

#### 3. Preferred Format
In terms of Section 7(9) of the RTI Act, 2005, I request that the data requested under items (1), (2), and (3) be provided in electronic form (machine-readable digital format on a CD/DVD/USB drive or via email download link) to prevent unnecessary expense and physical paper waste.
