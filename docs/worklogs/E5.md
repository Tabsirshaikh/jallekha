# E5 — under-served field-check screen

Branch `feat/e5-underserved`, based on `feat/e3-growth` so the E2/E3 inputs and E1 repair are present. The E5 configuration and service draft were already untracked on the branch at start; they were reviewed, hardened, tested, and completed here.

## Findings

- E2 contains 145 historical ward rows but has zero location-validated Census-to-polygon assignments; its 134 number-only links remain provisional, with ambiguous and missing source assignments also present.
- HMWSSB tanker CSVs are monthly division/section booking and delivery counts. E1/E3 contain no verified section-to-ward transfer, so those observations cannot be treated as ward booking counts.
- E3 has 145 building-index ratios, but each carries the engineering annual-variability review flag. E5 exposes this context, never uses it to increase household exposure or rank areas.
- Consequently, real-data modeling must be withheld. The generated operational artifact puts all 145 rows in `DATA_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and produces no ward field-check candidates. A field-check flag would be a hypothesis, not evidence of proven neglect.
- A low observed-booking ratio cannot by itself identify hidden demand, under-reporting probability, service failure, or corruption. Only later officer field observations can establish ground truth.

## Implementation

- `backend/app/services/underserved.py`: strict evidence gates, comparable-cohort checks, leave-one-area-out peers, need-side thresholds, cross-variant agreement, and plain-language reasons.
- `backend/app/services/underserved_models.py`: optional negative-binomial and BYM2 / shrinkage BYM2 posteriors; explicit graph review provenance, training-only transforms/count likelihood, deterministic K-fold out-of-fold predictions, posterior diagnostic gate, and reference-ratio intervals. In-sample scores cannot enter flags. The reference mean has the area effect set to zero; it is not a reporting-propensity estimate.
- `scripts/evidence/build_underserved.py` and `verify_underserved.py`: deterministic E2/E3 join, fail-closed 145-row review queue, provenance hashes, and no synthetic data in operational output.
- `backend/tests/test_e5_underserved.py`: real-input gate assertions, synthetic peer/flag behavior, cross-validation leakage checks, graph invariants, Bayesian NB/BYM2 smoke fits, and reproducible artifact checks. Synthetic observations are test-only.
- `.github/workflows/p1-e5.yml`: task-specific CI; `tasks.py test` runs the offline E5 verifier.

## Validation / limits

The input blocker state, not model performance, is the result for Hyderabad today: 0 eligible areas, 0 real model scores, 0 field-check candidates, and 145 review rows. There are no field-check labels, so real-world accuracy, precision/recall, calibration, and impact remain unavailable. Model tests only show that the synthetic method paths execute and obey declared contracts; they do not validate the method for Hyderabad.

Threshold quantiles, priors, graph choices, and two-year staleness limit are configurable engineering policy, not entitlements or validated service standards. The spatial graph supplied to BYM2 must be reviewed for the relevant geography version before operational use. Do not merge section data, infer current household totals from Open Buildings, or use a flag to alter water allocation.

## Handoff

E5 is a safe review queue and testable screening method, not a live under-reporting detector yet. Reconsider real model activation only after location-validated Census inputs, an authoritative section-to-ward transfer, comparable booking periods and a reviewed spatial graph are available. E6 may visualize the review/data-quality state; it must not render unavailable scores as zero.
