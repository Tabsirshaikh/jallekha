# E3 — Open Buildings temporal growth signal

Branch feat/e3-growth; independent sibling to feat/e4-equity, based on the E1 repair PR #9.

## Findings and scope
Fetched all 80 public Google raster tiles intersecting the available 145-feature historical polygon layer,
for June 30 each year 2016–2023. Tile manifests and 32m cached reductions are checked by SHA-256.
DataMeet layer vintage is not independently verified and it is incomplete versus the nominal 150 wards.

The Open Buildings band measures fractional building count, not people or households. It has ~4m effective
resolution though source rasters are 0.5m; confidence is uncalibrated, and Google documents temporal/spatial
jitter, cloud/tiling artifacts and missed small buildings. E3 reports a derived building-count index ratio,
not current households. Average reduction to a 32m grid multiplies the mean fractional value by its source
pixel-area factor and gates polygons with <99% raster footprint or valid-cell coverage. Tests / output keep
coverage and yearly instability visible. Compare 16m/64m grids before treating small differences as stable.

## Independent signals and failed validation paths
WorldPop 2020 India GeoTIFF HEAD reports 1,844,139,160 bytes and its host rejects HTTP range reads; a full
country download is not justified for this small extract. GHS-POP is available at five-year epochs (the 2025
value is projected); its periods/grid need harmonization before a defensible 2016–2023 cross-source range.
These sources are registered with explicit NOT_EXTRACTED status. No multi-source agreement score is fabricated.

Section bookings start in 2022, and no verified section-to-ward crosswalk exists. E1 repair found zero
location-validated Census-to-polygon assignments. Therefore the planned ward-building versus section-booking
validation and Census-household multiplier are blocked. Each ward keeps household/population adjustment null.

## Validation and output
P1:E3 CI uses committed reduced source tiles offline, checks original-archive hashes, exact feature/year counts,
rebuilds annual estimates, checks output hashes, and exercises coverage/missingness gates. Raw source files stay
unchanged. E3 summary reports usable ratios and stability counts from the generated artifact. The available 145 polygons
currently clear the 99% coverage gates; the artifact reports a median 2016–2023 building index ratio of 1.2701.
This is a derived model signal, not an independently validated change in households or people. All 145 polygons currently exceed the explicitly heuristic annual-variability review threshold, so E5 must treat the feature as review-only and must not prioritize from it alone.

## Handoff
Use growth as one dated spatial covariate. Do not scale Census households or assert validated section bookings.
E5 must expose missing/unstable growth and keep alternative-source comparisons unavailable until those rasters
and a real section/ward transfer become accessible and reviewed. E4 remains independently reviewable.
